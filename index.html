COMMISSION REQUIREMENTS

I need a clear reference sheet, what and anything you would like to add such as toggles, please be a descriptive as you can when giving me any details. If you don't have a clear reference piece, I can also use VRC screenshots. (Please send me the colors that were used to make the avatar.) Don't give me a reference to a stolen character!

PAYMENT

I accept payment before I begin any artwork that is to be drawn out. I will take PAYPAL and CASHAPP. I will not accept gift cards or games. I will DO payment plans but before I start, half of the payment must be paid! Any charge backs after completion of the commission will be BLACKLISTED from commissioning me from the future!

REVISIONS

2D: I allow as many changes during the sketch phase as the commissioner would like, I will make the sketch clean as I can. After the sketch phase I won't allow any changes to the pose or body shape! I will allow small accessory changes such as jewelry and other small items.

3D: I allow as many changes during the Texture phase as the commissioner would like, I will send screenshots and if any changes are needed I will do so but please try to ask for texture changes while I'm in that phase.

TIMEFRAME

Depending on how my queue looks, it should take around a week to a couple of days (depending on how complex the commission is). I give multiple updates from start to completion of the commission!

REFUND POLICY

Refunds are only allowed before and during the sketch phase (or texture phase for 3D), you will receive a 50% refund. Refunds are NOT allowed after the sketch or texture phase is completed and confirmed.
